# GoHaode Feedback & Community Hub 🇨🇳

[English](README.md) | [简体中文](README-zh.md)

Welcome to the official public issue tracker for **[GoHaode (gohaode.com)](https://gohaode.com)**.

GoHaode (Go好的) is a free, offline-first survival kit for foreign visitors to China: visa-free and 240-hour transit checks, big Chinese show-cards with pinyin for taxis, restaurants, hotels and doctors, payment and transport fixes, dual clocks, and emergency numbers. It is made by the GateHop team.

---

## 💡 How to Give Feedback

- 🛂 **Correct a visa or policy rule**: [Submit Policy Correction](https://github.com/jviewes-commit/gohaode-feedback/issues/new?template=01_policy_correction.md)  
  *Visa-free entry, 240-hour transit, Hainan, payment or transport rules. Please include an official source (NIA, MFA, embassy).*
- 🀄 **Fix or suggest a show-card**: [Submit Card Feedback](https://github.com/jviewes-commit/gohaode-feedback/issues/new?template=02_card_content.md)  
  *Wrong Chinese, pinyin or translation, or a phrase you needed and couldn't find.*
- 🐛 **Report a Bug**: [Open Bug Report](https://github.com/jviewes-commit/gohaode-feedback/issues/new?template=03_bug_report.md)  
  *Layout glitches, offline mode not working, wrong time zone, install problems.*
- 💡 **Suggest a Feature**: [Submit Feature Request](https://github.com/jviewes-commit/gohaode-feedback/issues/new?template=04_feature_request.md)

No GitHub account? Email [support@gohaode.com](mailto:support@gohaode.com).

---

## 🔍 How We Check Facts

- Visa and entry rules come only from official sources: the National Immigration Administration, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Chinese embassies.
- Border officers make the final decision on entry. GoHaode is guidance, not legal advice.
- Corrections are checked against the official notice before they ship. See [About GoHaode](https://gohaode.com/about/).

---

## 🔒 Privacy

- **No account, no ads.** Everything you save stays on your phone.
- **Anonymous visit counts only**, via Cloudflare Web Analytics: no cookies, no cross-site tracking.
- **Works offline** once opened, including the show-cards and emergency numbers.
- Please don't post passport numbers, phone numbers or booking references in issues.
- Full policy: [Privacy & Safety](https://gohaode.com/#/privacy)
